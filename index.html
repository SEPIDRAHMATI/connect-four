How to play
  1. Start: one player taps Create a new game and sends the invite link or 5-letter code to a friend. The friend opens the link (or enters the code) on their own device.
  2. Colors: the creator plays Red, the friend plays Yellow. Red moves first in game one; after that, the starting player alternates each round.
  3. Your turn: tap any column that isn't full. Your disc falls to the lowest empty spot in that column.
  4. Win: the first player to line up four of their discs in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins the round and scores a point.
  5. Draw: if all 42 spots fill up with no four-in-a-row, the round is a draw.
  6. Rematch: when a round ends, either player can tap Play again. Scores carry over until someone leaves.
Moves travel directly between the two browsers (peer-to-peer). No account needed.
